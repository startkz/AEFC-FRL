# AEFC Supplemental Experiment Protocol

This protocol governs the five supplemental experiments added after the authoritative native IEEE39 560-run Main Experiment. The Main Experiment is frozen and is not regenerated, retuned, or overwritten by any supplemental runner.

## Fixed provenance

- Main native evidence: `results/ieee39_joint20` and `results/ieee39_generated`.
- Native mapping policy: `paired-generator-physical-speed-v2`.
- Native sample time, attack window, base attack magnitudes, controller coefficients, and 20 seeds remain those in `configs/ieee39_joint20.json` unless the supplemental experiment explicitly varies the named factor.
- Supplemental native outputs are written only under `results/supplemental_native`.
- Mechanism-level fairness calibration is written only under `results/supplemental_mechanism`.
- All supplemental numeric claims must be generated by `analysis/make_supplemental_results.py` from raw traces and per-seed summaries.

## E1 — Native attack-intensity robustness

Joint-corruption scale `gamma` is evaluated at 0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5, and 2.0. Gamma=0 reuses the authoritative clean Main cell; gamma=1.0 is rerun solely as a reproduction guard and must reproduce the authoritative joint Main summaries for shared methods to a maximum absolute discrepancy below 1e-9. Methods are FedRL, RobustAgg, AEFC-no-PATBU, AEFC-no-Gate, and AEFC-Full. The primary interpretation is continuous degradation of minimum native margin, maximum belief distortion, maximum deployed-policy movement, shield intervention, and recovery time. A binary safety-violation gap is not required and must not be manufactured by retuning.

## E2 — Component-local PATBU and Gate attribution

PATBU is evaluated at the evidence-to-belief boundary using AEFC-Full versus AEFC-no-PATBU across gamma=0.5,1.0,1.5,2.0. The primary endpoint is maximum belief distortion; paired Wilcoxon tests use Holm correction across the four gamma levels.

Gate attribution uses a targeted unsafe learned-proposal stress before authorization, with blend alpha in 0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1.0. The downstream shield and post-shield corruption ordering remain unchanged. The primary endpoints are pooled false-authorization rate among dangerous proposals, gate authorization rate, and shield intervention workload. Final plant safety is secondary because the shield may mask an upstream Gate difference.

## E3 — Fairness-calibrated mechanism baselines

The original mechanism runner uses method-dependent RNG streams. E3 therefore uses method-independent common random numbers for FedRL, FedRL-Clip, RobustFedRL-Clip, and AEFC-Full over the same 20 seed identifiers under joint corruption. FedRL-Clip uses the AEFC adaptation learning rate and clipping norm but no PATBU, risk Gate, trust-gated deployment, robust aggregation, or shield. RobustFedRL-Clip additionally uses robust aggregation. The primary endpoints are belief distortion and deployed-policy movement; safety/recovery metrics are reported but AEFC is not required to dominate them.

## E4 — Cross-backend consistency

Absolute values from the reduced-order and native backends are not equated. Consistency is assessed using method-level Spearman ranking for belief distortion, policy movement, and continuous safety margin. A metric that is constant in either backend is reported as uninformative rather than assigned an artificial correlation.

## E5 — One-at-a-time native parameter sensitivity

AEFC-Full is evaluated with q_min in {0.10,0.18,0.30}, rho_max in {0.40,0.55,0.70}, and Delta_theta/trust-region radius in {0.020,0.035,0.060}. The authoritative default point is reused from the Main Experiment. Only one parameter varies at a time. The purpose is to characterize robustness and security-utility trade-offs; the default setting is not required to be numerically optimal.

## Statistical and claim discipline

- All continuous paired comparisons use matched seeds and paired Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
- Exact all-zero paired differences are assigned p=1 before multiplicity correction.
- Holm correction is applied within each declared comparison family.
- No attack magnitude, threshold, or parameter grid may be changed because an observed result is non-significant or visually weak.
- Negative, flat, or baseline-favorable results are retained and interpreted as such.
- Supplemental results qualify component attribution and robustness claims; they do not replace the authoritative Main Experiment.
